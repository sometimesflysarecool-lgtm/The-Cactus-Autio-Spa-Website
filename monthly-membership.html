FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a contract or long-term commitment?
No contracts, ever. You can cancel your membership at any time with no fees or penalties. We want you to stay because the service is worth it, not because you're locked in.
What's included in each membership visit?
Every membership visit includes a full interior detail — multi-step soil removal, carpet and upholstery treatment, interior wipe-down and sanitizing, leather and fabric care, glass and screen cleaning, UV protection for plastics, and floor mat rejuvenation. Exterior services are available as add-ons.
Can I change my membership frequency?
Yes. If your schedule or needs change, just reach out and we can switch you between monthly, bi-monthly, and quarterly plans at any time.
How does priority booking work?
Members are always scheduled ahead of one-time customers. When time slots are limited, you get first access to preferred windows. You'll never lose your spot to a new booking.
Can I add exterior services to my membership visits?
Absolutely. Exterior wash, wax, and other add-ons can be added to any membership visit. Just let us know when you book and we'll take care of it at the same visit.
How do I secure an appointment spot?
Submit a quote request and we'll reach out shortly. Once we connect, a 20% deposit is required to fully secure your appointment spot.
